# PRODUCT SPECIFICATION — STOCK INTELLIGENCE

## 1. Product Scope & Vision

`STOCK INTELLIGENCE` is an enterprise-grade AI-powered financial research and continuous monitoring platform designed specifically for U.S. Stocks and U.S. Mutual Funds (with future extension hooks for ETFs, European, and Indian markets).

### Core Differentiators
1. **Evidence-Based AI Synthesis**: All statements generated by the AI agent are backed by Tier 1-3 primary sources with verifiable inline citations.
2. **Management Execution Tracker**: Tracks executive claims over time, comparing stated goals against subsequent quarterly and annual results to evaluate management track records empirically.
3. **Dual Analysis Engine**: Treats equities and mutual funds as distinct asset classes with tailored analytical workflows, valuation models, and screening parameters.
4. **Controlled Multi-Agent System**: Utilizes a LangGraph Supervisor + Specialist Agent structure rather than a single uncontrolled chatbot prompt.
5. **Continuous Sentinel & "What Changed?" Intelligence**: Saves past research snapshots and automatically highlights fundamental, technical, news, and goal deviations when new data arrives.

## 4. Deep-Dive Feature Specifications

### 4.1. Management Execution Tracker
Tracks corporate commitments chronologically:
1. **Statement Extraction**: AI extracts forward-looking statements from earnings transcripts, 8-Ks, and investor presentations.
2. **Metadata Tagging**: Tags target metric/milestone, target timeframe, executive name, and source document.
3. **Empirical Verification**: Compares future 10-K/10-Q filing figures against the target.
4. **Status Classification**:
   - `ACHIEVED`: Verified by filing evidence.
   - `PROGRESSING`: On track according to interim quarterly evidence.
   - `MISSED`: Failed to meet stated target within specified timeframe.
   - `UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED`: Insufficient filing data available.

### 4.2. Business Segments & Corporate Structure Visualizer
- **Segment Breakdown**: Visualizes revenue and margin contribution per business segment over 1, 3, and 5 years.
- **Subsidiary Graph**: Interactive node-link graph mapping ownership structures:
  - `Parent Entity` -> `100% Owned Subsidiary`
  - `Parent Entity` -> `Joint Venture (X% stake)`
  - `Parent Entity` -> `Minority Investment`

### 4.3. Programmatic Technical Analysis Engine
Calculated strictly via deterministic Python code (Pandas/TA-Lib) without LLM guesswork:
- **Trend Indicators**: SMA (20, 50, 200), EMA (9, 21), Golden Cross / Death Cross signals.
- **Momentum Indicators**: RSI (14-period), MACD (12, 26, 9) signal line & histogram.
- **Volatility & Volume**: Bollinger Bands (20, 2), ATR (14), VWAP, OBV, Volume spikes (>2x 20-day average).
- **Levels**: Support & Resistance levels identified via local price minima/maxima and pivot points.

### 4.4. Stock & Mutual Fund Screeners
- **Visual Filters**: Multi-variable filter builders (e.g., `Market Cap > $10B`, `Revenue Growth > 15%`, `ROE > 18%`, `Debt/Equity < 0.8`, `FCF Yield > 4%`).
- **Natural Language Screener**:
  - Example prompt: *"Find profitable U.S. tech companies with over 15% revenue growth, low debt, and P/E under 30."*
  - **Filter Translation Confirmation**: The system displays interpreted JSON rules (e.g., `{"sector": "Technology", "rev_growth_3y": {">$gt": 0.15}, "net_margin": {">$gt": 0}, "pe_ratio": {">$lt": 30}}`) for user confirmation prior to execution.

### 4.5. Mutual Fund Analysis Engine
Dedicated workspace for mutual funds evaluating:
- Fund Category, AUM, Expense Ratio, Turnover Rate, Portfolio Concentration Top 10.
- Sector & Asset Class Allocations.
- Historical Performance vs. Benchmark Index (1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y CAGR).
- Risk Metrics: Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Beta, Standard Deviation, Alpha.
- Manager Tenure & Strategy Audit.
- Side-by-side Fund Comparison Matrix (up to 4 funds).

### 4.6. News Ingestion & Event Deduplication
- **23 Specialized Categories**: Earnings, Product, Partnership, Acquisition, Merger, CEO/Management, Regulation, Legal, Government, Customer, Supply Chain, Debt, Dividend, Buyback, Layoffs, Expansion, Competition, Technology, AI, Cybersecurity, International, Macro.
- **Smart Grouping**: Clusters multiple news sources discussing the exact same story into a single Event Card displaying primary headline, source list, key takeaways, and affected business segments.

### 4.7. AI Research Report Generation Workflow
Clicking **"AI RESEARCH"** triggers a full multi-agent investigation generating a standardized 22-section report:
```text
1. Executive Summary               12. Technical Analysis
2. Company Overview                 13. Latest News & Event Grouping
3. Business Model & Moat            14. Historical Important Developments
4. Product Breakdown                15. Upcoming Events Calendar
5. Segment Analysis                 16. Management Assessment
6. Financial Performance            17. Management Execution Track Record
7. Growth Trajectory                18. Subsidiaries & Corporate Structure
8. Margins & Profitability          19. Competitive Intelligence
9. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation   20. Risk Matrix & Mitigants
10. Balance Sheet Strength          21. Bull / Base / Bear Scenarios
11. Valuation Analysis              22. Verified Sources & Citations
```

### 4.8. "What Changed?" Delta Analysis
Compares two research report runs (e.g., Run A on Oct 1 vs Run B on Nov 15) and highlights:
- `NEW`: Newly identified risks, management goals, or news events.
- `IMPROVED`: Revenue growth acceleration, margin expansion, debt reduction.
- `DETERIORATED`: Margin compression, missed management goals, regulatory investigations.
- `UNCHANGED`: Core business model and thesis pillars.

## 5. User Persona Scenarios

### Persona A: Equity Research Analyst
Uses the platform to pull SEC filings, inspect 10-year financial metrics, run RAG queries on 10-K footnotes, analyze management execution against past statements, and export evidence-backed research reports.

### Persona B: Active Retail Investor
Uses the natural language screener to find high-quality growth stocks, sets up custom alerts (e.g., price movements > 5%, management changes, earnings dates), and reads the Daily AI Briefing every morning.

### Persona C: Portfolio Manager
Monitors a 30-stock watchlist, uses the "What Changed?" feature to track quarter-over-quarter fundamental shifts, and reviews mutual fund risk metrics for client asset allocation.
